Water in the house in Ellicott City: first steps

Ellicott City is an unincorporated community in Howard County and the county seat. At the 2020 census its census-designated place held 75,947 people. Those homes range from rowhouses on Main Street to subdivisions along US 40, and the water problems vary just as widely.

If water is coming in right now, close the main valve if a pipe is the cause, keep out of the water if it reaches outlets and call the 24/7 line. Describe the street, the source and which rooms are wet. Many Ellicott City calls begin with a basement, since the town sits in a valley.

A valley town that floods fast

The Patapsco River borders Ellicott City on the east and north and forms the Baltimore County line. The Tiber River forms the valley that Main Street follows. Water from the hills above arrives in the valley quickly, which is why the town is known for flash floods.

The National Weather Service reported that more than 6 inches of rain fell near Ellicott City in 90 minutes or less on July 30, 2016, and that the Patapsco rose just over 13 feet in 100 minutes. On May 27, 2018, 9 inches or more fell near and southwest of Old Town Ellicott City. These are the events that shape how residents here think about water.

Howard County permits and utilities

Ellicott City is in Howard County, not Baltimore County. Howard County's Licenses and Permits Division handles building permits. If you are on public sewer, Howard County says to call its Bureau of Utilities before a plumber. The rules differ from the Baltimore County ones used for Catonsville, so confirm which office applies to your address.

Howard County has also been building flood mitigation. Its North Tunnel is under construction with substantial completion expected in Fall 2027, and the H-7, Quaker Mill and H-4 ponds are complete. The Maryland Avenue culvert is in final permitting and design. These projects do not remove the need to protect your own property.

Older buildings, newer subdivisions

Ellicott City's Historic District was designated in 1973, and the B and O station dates from 1830. Buildings that old often have stone foundations, original plumbing and shallow cellars. Newer houses use poured foundations and finished basements. Tell the dispatcher which kind of building you have, since it changes the drying plan.

The town has about 27,657 housing units, and our work covers nearby Oella, Ilchester and Daniels as well, which share the 21043 ZIP code. Neighbors we can also reach include Halethorpe, Windsor Mill, Gwynn Oak and Pikesville, though those are outside the ZIP codes listed above.
