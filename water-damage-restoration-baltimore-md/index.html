Baltimore City is not Baltimore County

Catonsville sits in Baltimore County, and Baltimore City is a separate jurisdiction that borders it on the east. That matters for water. The city runs its own permits, its own 311 reporting and its own cleanup programs, and the county runs different ones. Know which side of the line your building is on before you call an office.

Question.Baltimore City.Baltimore County.
Report a sewer backup.Call 311.Call the county sewer line.
Building permits.Department of Housing and Community Development.Permits, Approvals and Inspections.
Cleanup help.SOS Cleanup Program for eligible basement sewage backups.Not the same program.
Water service.City provides it.City also supplies county water.

Baltimore County itself says Baltimore City is responsible for water service in the county. The city's reservoirs are Liberty, Loch Raven and Prettyboy. A main break outside your house is a city job, and the damage inside is ours.

What the city program does and does not do

Baltimore City's SOS Cleanup Program offers free cleanup for eligible basement sewage backups, and residents report the problem to 311. The city also has an Expedited Reimbursement program, and a household gets one program, not both. Ask 311 which applies before you hire anyone.

For other water, such as burst pipes, appliance floods or roof leaks, the city program does not apply and you handle it through your insurer and a restoration company. Our sewage backup page explains the contaminated-water process.

Rowhouses, apartments and offices

Baltimore buildings often share walls, floors and plumbing stacks. A leak in one unit reaches the next, and a backup in a shared line affects several basements at once. Drying a rowhouse means working around party walls, so the plan has to be built with neighbors in mind.

Offices and shops in the city face the same issue on a larger scale. See commercial restoration for large-building drying, and basement cleanup for the lower levels that many city homes have.

Whether the water came from a pipe or from the sewer, the ANSI/IICRC S500 category rules decide what is dried and what is removed. Tell the dispatcher the source so the plan starts on the right category.
