Water Damage Service Area Around Catonsville, MD

We are based in Catonsville and also take calls from Ellicott City, Elkridge and Baltimore. Coverage depends on your address, so call with your street and ZIP code and a dispatcher will confirm before anything else.

Home base: Catonsville

Catonsville is an unincorporated community in Baltimore County. The 2020 census counted 44,701 people and 17,189 housing units, and its main ZIP code is 21228. The Patapsco River forms its western and southern edge. Neighbors include Woodlawn to the north, Baltimore City to the east, Arbutus to the southeast, Ilchester to the southwest and Ellicott City to the west.

Our address is 5748 Baltimore National Pike, Catonsville, MD 21228. Crews are often out on jobs, so please call before you visit. For emergencies, call the 24/7 line instead of driving over.

These pages differ because the rules differ. Howard County has its own permit office and its Bureau of Utilities handles sewer calls on public sewer. Baltimore City has 311 and the SOS Cleanup Program. Baltimore County, where Catonsville sits, has its own permits and sewer backup service.

Catonsville neighborhoods and landmarks

Catonsville is made of many small neighborhoods, among them Academy Heights, Colonial Gardens, Ingleside, Westview Park, Summit Park, part of Historic Catonsville, and Winters Lane. When you call, naming your neighborhood and the nearest cross street helps the dispatcher place you quickly, even if your mailing address uses a different town name.

Local landmarks help too. Castle Thunder dates from 1787, the Trolley Line No. 9 Trail runs through the community, Benjamin Banneker Historical Park is nearby, and the University of Maryland, Baltimore County and the CCBC Catonsville campus are close. If you are near one of them, say so.

A single storm can hit several of these places at once, and a pipe failure can happen anywhere. Phone numbers and offices for each agency change, so we do not print them here. The dispatcher will give you the right one for your address when you call (667) 767-7092.

None of this changes the first steps. Stop the water if you can, stay out of water near electrical equipment and call the line. It changes who else you call afterward, and we point you to the right office on the phone.

Why we do not list a drive time

A published arrival time sounds helpful and often is not true. Traffic on US 40, I-95 and the Baltimore Beltway changes by the hour, and a crew may be finishing another job. What we can tell you is whether a same-day response is possible when a crew is available, and we say so plainly if it is not.

If you are comparing companies, call us and ask exactly what you will be told on the first call. We will answer plainly, and the services overview shows what each situation involves.

That honesty extends to the edges of our area. If your address is farther out, the dispatcher will say so rather than promise something the schedule cannot deliver, and may suggest you call a closer company for the first hours.

Other places we can talk about

People also call from places such as Woodstock, Harmans, Brooklandville and Stevenson. Those do not have their own pages and are not on our listed ZIP codes, so we handle them case by case. Call with your address and the dispatcher will tell you honestly whether a crew can reach you.
